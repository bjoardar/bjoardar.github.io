Teaching

Recipient of the Teaching Excellence Award, University of Houston (2025).

  • ECE 5346/6346: VLSI University of Houston · Spring and Fall 2023, Spring and Fall 2024, Fall 2025, Fall 2026

    Hands-on circuit design. Students simulate circuits and create schematics and layouts using industry-standard tools.

    LTspice Cadence Virtuoso (schematic and layout) Cadence Genus (synthesis) Cadence Innovus (place and route) Cadence Pegasus (physical verification) Cadence Quantus (parasitic extraction)

    Example projects: DRAM design, ALU design

  • ECE 3331: C Programming University of Houston · Spring 2025, Spring 2026 (about 100 students each term)

    Covers C programming fundamentals and data structures. Students work in a Linux-based virtual machine environment.

    Example project: Stock trading simulator

  • ECE 590-7061: Machine Learning-based Hardware Design Methodologies Duke University · Fall 2021
